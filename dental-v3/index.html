book a calm visit
  • numbing: you feel a small pinch, then a numb area; you remember everything; drive after: yes; who watches you: your dentist and an assistant.
  • laughing gas: you feel relaxed, a little floaty; you remember most of it; drive after: yes, after a short rest; who watches you: your dentist, the whole time.
  • calming pill: you feel sleepy and calm; you remember some of it; drive after: no, bring a ride; who watches you: your dentist and a team member.
  • IV sedation: you feel very drowsy; you remember little or none; drive after: no, an adult takes you home; who watches you: a team member who only watches you.
